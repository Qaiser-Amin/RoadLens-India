TRANSPARENCY & COVERAGE

Understand the numbers.

This working web interface uses verified extracts from official MoRTH reports. It complements the Tableau construction specification; no Tableau workbook interactions have been tested.

What is available?

State totals and road types: 2020–2023. Road-user fatalities and reported traffic violations: 2022–2023. Unreported values remain unavailable. “All years” adds reported counts; it is not an annual average.

Severity & risk

Severity = deaths per 100 accidents, calculated only from paired observations. It is not crash probability. Two-wheeler accident denominators are unavailable, so two-wheeler severity is unavailable. High risk is the intersection of Top-N accident states and states above your severity threshold, using complete paired observations. This is an exploratory classification, not an official designation.

Geography & comparisons

The illustrative map uses third-party district geometry dissolved to 36 state/UT shapes. It is not an official administrative or historical boundary map. State names and definitions follow reporting tables. Some Kerala “Other” road-user figures in 2023 are included in Pedestrians. Cause attribution is not proof of causality.
